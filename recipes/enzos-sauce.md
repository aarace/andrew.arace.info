# Enzo's Sauce

Created as a dupe to Raising Cane's sauce, by researching other's attempts in youtube videos and blog posts.

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup mayo (recommended brand: Mike's amazing)
- 1/4 cup ketchup
- 1 teaspoon worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic salt
- 1/2 teaspoon fine ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika

### Directions
Mix all ingredients together thoroughly.

Use immediately, or refrigerate in a sealed container.
